Questions

What people ask before they apply.

Do I need followers?

No. Brands pay UGC creators for the video file and post it on their own accounts and ads. Your follower count rarely comes up.

Do I need a camera or special gear?

No. A phone from the last 4 to 5 years, daylight from a window and a quiet room are enough. We'll talk about a $10 to $20 mic only if you want one.

Will I earn $1,000 a month?

That is the goal we build toward, and I cannot promise it. Some people land a first paid order during the program, others need a few more months of pitching. By week 8 you will have a portfolio, a pitch that works and a price list.

I live in a different time zone. What about the calls?

Calls are on Thursdays at 7 pm WIB, which is Jakarta time (UTC+7): 12 pm in London, 1 pm in Berlin, 7 am in New York. Every call is recorded. You can send questions before the call and watch the answers later.

How is this different from a video course?

A course gives you lessons. Here you also get someone who checks your videos and pitches each week, in a group small enough that she knows your name.

What happens after I join the waitlist?

Cimey reads every entry and replies within 48 hours. When the start date is ready, she tells the waitlist first. You then have a few days to confirm your seat and get the payment link. If the group doesn't fit you, she'll say so honestly. Until then you pay nothing.

I speak Bahasa Indonesia. Is there a group in Indonesian?

Yes, with its own page and a price in rupiah. Leave your email below and you'll get the link when it opens.
