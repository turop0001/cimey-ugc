FAQ

Do you need my physical product?

For apps, SaaS and digital products, no. For physical products, I work with brands that can ship within Southeast Asia.

Can you make content in Bahasa Indonesia?

Yes. Native Indonesian content is ideal for brands entering a market of 280M people.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on the concept, length, number of videos, usage rights and timeline. After a free written consultation I send a clear custom quote.

How do payments work?

50% upfront and 50% on delivery via PayPal, cryptocurrency or Indonesian bank transfer, or through Fiverr or Collabstr if you prefer.

Do you accept free stay or products instead of payment?

Thank you for the kind offer! My creative work is always paid, so I work for a fee. For hotels and travel brands, I would be glad to have accommodation and travel costs covered in addition to the fee, but not in place of it.

Can you post on my channels or run ads from my account?

Yes. Whitelisting and Spark Ads are available as an add-on.
